/ The experience

Think in angles.
See the path.

Light travels in straight lines. Mirrors change its direction. Your challenge is to read the board, understand the available moves and find the sequence that sends the beam exactly where it needs to go.

AFTERGLOW keeps the rules easy to grasp while asking you to think a few moves ahead. Every rotation matters. Every route is a small logic problem waiting to be solved.

01 / OBSERVE

Read the board

Locate the laser, target and mirrors. Work out how the light can travel before you make a move.

02 / ROTATE

Shape the route

Rotate mirrors to change the beam's direction and build a path across the board.

03 / SOLVE

Find the answer

Make your moves count and guide the light to the target to complete the puzzle.
