Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026 · Last updated: 4 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NAVTS ("we", "us" or "our") handles information in the AFTERGLOW Android game (package name: com.navts.afterglow) and when you contact us for support. It is based on the reviewed AFTERGLOW 0.7.1 source project and the 0.7.2 release change, which adds only an in-app link to this policy. It applies to the app version described below and may be updated if the app's features, code or included software components change.

In short

AFTERGLOW 0.7.2 is designed to run offline. It does not ask you to create an account, does not request Android permissions, and does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other data-collection SDKs in the reviewed source. Your game progress and preferences are saved locally on your device. The app does not send that game data to NAVTS.

1. Information handled by the app

Game information stored on your device. The app uses local WebView storage to save gameplay information such as campaign progress, unlocked levels, scores and best scores, daily-game results, milestones, run state, player statistics, and preferences such as sound and display settings. This information is stored locally so the game can remember your progress between sessions. It is not uploaded to a NAVTS server by the reviewed app.

No account or profile registration. The reviewed version has no sign-in, account creation, social login or cloud synchronisation feature.

No collection through app permissions. The reviewed Android manifest declares no Android permissions. The app does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos/files or other device permissions.

2. Network access, analytics and advertising

The reviewed app build does not declare the Android INTERNET permission. Its WebView is configured to block network loads and uses bundled local game assets. The source project contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, attribution or in-app billing SDKs. The app does not transmit gameplay data, device identifiers or usage analytics to NAVTS or third-party analytics/ad providers.

The game includes a link to the NAVTS website. If you choose to open that link, it is handed to your device's external browser; the website is outside the game app and may be subject to separate website, browser, network-provider and hosting practices. The game does not attach your saved game data to that link.

3. Storage, retention and deletion

Your game information remains in the app's local storage on your device until you delete it. You can use the in-game profile reset control to clear saved AFTERGLOW progress and profile information. You can also clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app; doing so may permanently remove local progress. The reviewed build disables Android app-data backup and does not provide cloud recovery or cross-device synchronisation.

4. Support messages

If you email helpnavts@gmail.com, NAVTS will receive the email address and the information you choose to include, such as your message, device model, Android version, app version, screenshots or diagnostic details you voluntarily provide. We use this information to respond to your request, investigate the reported issue and maintain reasonable support records. Please do not send passwords, payment details or unrelated sensitive information.

Support correspondence is handled through the email service used by the sender and recipient. Email service providers may process message and delivery metadata under their own terms and privacy policies. We retain support correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, maintain relevant support records, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. You may ask us to delete support correspondence by emailing the address above; some records may need to be retained where the law requires it.

5. Sharing and sale of information

NAVTS does not sell personal information. The reviewed game does not send locally stored game data to third parties. We do not share app gameplay information with advertisers or analytics providers. Information you voluntarily include in a support email is processed by the email services involved in sending and receiving it and may be disclosed if required by law.

6. Security

Game data is stored locally within the app's WebView storage. The reviewed Android configuration disables app-data backup, blocks WebView network loads and disallows cleartext traffic. No method of storage or electronic communication is completely risk-free, so please keep your device secured and avoid including sensitive information in support messages.

7. Children and personal information

The reviewed app does not ask players to submit personal information or create accounts. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to NAVTS through a support email, contact us so we can review and address the request.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You can delete locally stored game progress using the in-game profile reset, Android app storage controls or by uninstalling the app. For questions or requests concerning a support email or this policy, contact helpnavts@gmail.com. We will respond in accordance with applicable privacy and data-protection law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes or when legal requirements change. The current version and its effective date will be published on this page. If a future version introduces accounts, online services, analytics, advertising, purchases or other data practices, this policy will be updated to describe them before those practices are introduced.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact NAVTS at helpnavts@gmail.com.
