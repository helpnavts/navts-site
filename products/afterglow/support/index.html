Support

Need help with AFTERGLOW? Contact NAVTS by email. We welcome bug reports, gameplay questions and feedback.

Contact support

helpnavts@gmail.com

Email is the official support channel for AFTERGLOW.

To help us investigate an issue

  • Your device model and Android version
  • The AFTERGLOW app version
  • The level or mode where the issue occurred
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead
  • Steps to reproduce the issue, if known
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if useful

Before contacting support

If the game does not respond, close and reopen it and check that you are using the latest version available to you. Please note that clearing app storage or uninstalling the game can delete locally saved progress.

Privacy and safety

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers or unrelated sensitive personal information. Information you include in an email will be handled as described in the AFTERGLOW Privacy Policy.
