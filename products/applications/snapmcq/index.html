SnapMCQ is an MCQ practice app for Indian entrance exams, starting with NEET UG. Every question comes with a worked solution, and a question you have answered is never served to you as new again.

Availability

SnapMCQ is in closed testing on Android. A public release on Google Play will be announced here.

What you get

  • Chapter-wise practice for NEET UG Class 11 Physics, Chemistry and Biology, with Class 12 and JEE Main following
  • Step-by-step solutions for every question
  • Timed tests with the exam's marking scheme
  • A revision queue that brings wrong answers back after a few days
  • Progress analytics by subject, chapter and topic

Built for students

Sign in with just your email, no password. You can delete your account and data in the app whenever you like.
