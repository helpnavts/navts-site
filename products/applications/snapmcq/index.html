/ The idea

Fresh questions.
Honest feedback.

Most practice apps let questions repeat until your score looks better than your understanding. SnapMCQ is built the other way round: you meet each question as new exactly once, you see the reasoning straight away, and the mistakes you make are brought back later, when you are ready to test yourself again.

Every question is written for the NEET UG syllabus and checked chapter by chapter before it reaches students. It is a practice companion for your textbooks, not a replacement for them.

01

Never see a repeat

A question you have answered is never served to you as new again. Fresh questions only, until a topic is truly finished.

02

A solution for every question

Step-by-step reasoning, and why each wrong option is wrong, right after you answer.

03

Timed tests, real marking

Each exam uses its own marking scheme, such as +4 / −1 / 0, shown before you start and applied exactly.

04

Revision that returns

Questions you got wrong come back after a few days, and are retired once you answer them right in a row.

05

Analytics by chapter

First-try accuracy, weak topics and days practised, so you spend time where it counts.

06

Modules of your own

Combine chapters, choose difficulty and question source, and save the module to reuse.
