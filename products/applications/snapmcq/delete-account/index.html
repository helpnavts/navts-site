Delete your account

You can permanently delete your SnapMCQ account and the data linked to it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

Open SnapMCQ, go to Profile, choose Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm. Deletion happens immediately.

By email

If you can no longer open the app, email helpnavts@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We will delete it and confirm by reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profile (name, date of birth, optional mobile number), your answers, scores, test attempts, bookmarks, saved practice modules, revision schedule, institute link, question reports, support tickets, your consent record (including any guardian details) and your sign-in record.

What may remain

Support emails already delivered to a mailbox stay in that mailbox. Nothing else is kept after deletion.

See also the Privacy Policy.
