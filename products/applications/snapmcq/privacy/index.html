Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026 · Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who we are

SnapMCQ is a practice app for NEET UG and JEE Main preparation, a product of NAVTS ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have. Contact for any privacy question or complaint: helpnavts@gmail.com.

What we collect

Account data: your email address (used to sign you in with a one-time code), your name and date of birth, and, only if you choose to give it, your mobile number.

Study data: the exams you select, the questions you answer, your answers, scores, test attempts, bookmarks, saved practice modules and revision schedule.

Institute data: if you enter an institute code, we record that link and any premium period it gives you.

Messages: question reports and support tickets you send us, and our replies.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, and we do not use advertising identifiers.

Why we use it

To sign you in, show only questions you have not answered before, mark your tests, show your progress and analytics, and bring wrong answers back for revision.

To answer your support requests and fix wrong or unclear questions you report.

To keep the service secure and to prevent copying of our question bank.

We do not sell your data and we do not show third-party ads.

Date of birth and young users

We ask for your date of birth to know your age. If you are under 18, please use SnapMCQ with the knowledge and permission of a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete the account at any time.

Who handles your data

Our service providers store and process data on our behalf: Supabase (database and sign-in, hosted in Mumbai, India) and our email provider, which delivers sign-in codes and support replies. They may only use the data to provide these services to us.

If you were given an institute code, the institute that issued it may see that you joined using its code. We do not share your answers or scores with an institute unless the product page you use says so.

Content protection

To protect our question bank, the app blocks screenshots and screen recording on supported phones and shows your account email faintly on screen. This is not used to track you.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, answers, tests, bookmarks, tickets and sign-in record are deleted permanently. Support emails already sent may remain in the mailboxes they were sent to.

Your choices and rights

You can view and correct your name and details in the app, and you can delete your account and all your data in the app: Profile, then Delete my account (web page: https://navts.tech/products/applications/snapmcq/delete-account/). You may also write to us at helpnavts@gmail.com to access, correct or delete your data, or to withdraw consent. We reply within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our reply you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to it is limited by database rules, and answers to questions are checked on our server. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your email account safe.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and tell you in the app for important changes.
