Privacy Policy

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NAVTS ("NAVTS", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores and protects your personal data when you use the SnapMCQ app, a practice app for NEET UG and JEE Main preparation. NAVTS decides why and how your data is used, so under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") NAVTS is the Data Fiduciary for SnapMCQ.

By creating an account and ticking the consent box you agree to this policy. Your consent is recorded together with the policy version and the date. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

Personal data we collect

Account data (required): your email address, your full name and your date of birth.

Optional data: your mobile number, an institute code if your coaching institute gave you one, and your ranking choices (whether you take part, the nickname you pick and whether your full name or nickname is shown).

Study data: the exams you choose, questions you answer, your answers, whether they were right, time taken, tests you take and your scores, bookmarks, saved practice modules and your revision schedule.

Messages: question reports and support tickets you send, and our replies.

Consent record: the policy version you accepted and when, and, if you are under 18, your parent or guardian's name and email address.

Technical data: your sign-in session on your device. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or advertising identifier, and we do not use analytics or advertising software in the app.

Why we use your data (purposes)

To create your account and sign you in with a one-time email code.

To serve questions you have not answered before, check your answers, mark tests, and show your progress, weak topics and revision reminders.

To answer your support requests and to review and correct questions you report.

If you link an institute code, to let that institute see your progress for coaching and teaching purposes, as described under "Institutes and coaching centres" below.

If you choose to take part, to place you in rankings for tests, as described under "Rankings" below.

To apply an institute code and any premium period it gives you.

To keep the service secure, stop abuse and protect our question bank from copying.

To meet legal obligations. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

Children and young users

SnapMCQ is meant for students aged 13 and above. We do not allow accounts for children under 13.

If you are under 18, the DPDP Act requires the consent of a parent or lawful guardian. At sign-up we therefore ask for your parent or guardian's name and email address and a confirmation that they agree to this policy and to your use of SnapMCQ. We never show advertising to users under 18 and we do not track or profile them for advertising. The only sharing of a young user's progress with anyone else is with their own coaching institute, and only if the student links that institute's code, as described below.

A parent or guardian can write to helpnavts@gmail.com at any time to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of the account, or to withdraw consent. If we learn that an account was opened without the required consent, we will delete it.

Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent personal data. We use service providers (Data Processors) who handle data only on our instructions: Supabase (database and sign-in, servers in Mumbai, India) and our email provider (to send sign-in codes and support replies).

If you link an institute code, that institute can see your name and your learning progress, as described under "Institutes and coaching centres". We never give an institute your email address, mobile number, date of birth or your individual answers to questions. Other students never see your data except your ranking name if you choose to take part in rankings.

We may disclose data when the law requires it, for example to a court or government authority acting under Indian law.

Institutes and coaching centres

Using an institute code is your choice. A student who signs up without a code is never visible to any institute.

If you link a code, the institute (for example your coaching centre) can see, from the date you link: your name, how many questions you practise and when you were last active, your accuracy, your weaker chapters, and your test scores and attempts. This helps the institute teach and support you. The institute cannot see your email address, mobile number, date of birth or your individual answers. Institute staff are given dashboard access only for their own students.

We tell you this before you link a code and ask you to tick a box to confirm. You can unlink at any time in Profile. Unlinking ends the institute's access immediately. The institute may keep only the figures it already recorded for its own teaching purposes, under its own policy, and it can no longer see new activity. Premium access that came with the code continues until its end date.

The institute decides how it uses what it sees about its own students and is responsible for telling you and, for students under 18, for obtaining the consent of your parent or guardian before it gives you its code. Our agreement with each institute requires this, and requires the institute to use student data only for teaching and student support. NAVTS stays responsible for the data held in SnapMCQ.

An institute may also give its students private tests and questions that other students do not see.

Rankings

Rankings are optional. Nothing is shown about you in any ranking unless you choose to take part. You can take part in the all-India ranking, in your institute's ranking, in both, or in neither, and you can change your mind in Profile, Ranking choices.

If you take part, other participants may see your rank and score on a test, shown under the name you pick: a nickname, or your full name if you are 18 or older. Users under 18 are always shown by nickname only. A rank is shown only when enough people take part, and the first attempt at a test is the one that counts.

An institute can ask that its adult students take part in that institute's ranking. In that case adult students linked to that institute are included in it, but never in the all-India ranking unless they choose to. Students under 18 are never included without their own choice.

Where data is stored

Your data is stored on servers in India. Our email provider may process email messages outside India solely to deliver them.

Content protection and your screen

To protect our question bank, the app blocks screenshots and screen recording on supported phones and shows your account email faintly across the screen. This is a deterrent against copying. It is not used to track you or sent anywhere.

How long we keep data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. If you unlink from an institute, we keep a record of when you linked and unlinked for our own audit purposes. When you delete your account, your profile, answers, tests, bookmarks, saved modules, support tickets, consent record and sign-in record are permanently deleted straight away.

Emails we have already sent stay in the mailboxes they were delivered to. Ordinary backups kept by our hosting provider are overwritten on their normal cycle. If we do not hear from you, accounts that stay inactive for three years may be deleted after we give notice.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to: get a summary of the personal data we hold and how it is used; correct or complete your data; erase your data; withdraw consent; have your grievance addressed; and nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or cannot act for yourself.

You can correct your name in the app by contacting support, and you can delete your account and data yourself in the app: Profile, then Delete my account. The same is possible on the web: https://navts.tech/products/applications/snapmcq/delete-account/.

For any other request write to helpnavts@gmail.com from your registered email address. We will reply within 30 days.

Grievance officer and complaints

If you have a concern about your data, write to the NAVTS Grievance Officer at helpnavts@gmail.com. We will acknowledge within 7 days and resolve within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is functioning.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to the database is controlled by rules that let you see only your own data. Correct answers are checked on our server. We limit staff access to what is needed for support and content review.

If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires. No system is completely secure, so please protect your email account, which controls sign-in.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The version and date are shown at the top of this page. For important changes we will tell you in the app and ask for your consent again where the law needs it.
