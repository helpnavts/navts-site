Support

Need help with SnapMCQ? You can write to NAVTS by email, or use the Help and support option inside the app (Profile, then Help and support) to send a ticket and read our reply.

Contact support

helpnavts@gmail.com

Email is the official support channel. We aim to reply within a few working days.

Found a wrong or unclear question?

Use the flag button on any question in the app and pick a reason. Reports go straight to our content team and are reviewed.

Sign-in problems

SnapMCQ signs you in with a one-time code sent to your email. Check your spam folder, make sure you typed the address correctly, and request a new code if it has expired. If it still does not arrive, email us from the same address.

To help us investigate

  • Your phone model and Android version
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened
  • A screenshot, if useful. Note that the app blocks screenshots of questions, so describe the question by its topic instead.

Privacy and safety

Please do not send passwords or payment details. Information you send is handled as described in the Privacy Policy.
