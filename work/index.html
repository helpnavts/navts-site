Case study 01

Kesar Hospitality
Services

Hospitality servicesSolapur · MaharashtraVisit live site ↗
kesarhospitality.com

Before this project, Kesar Hospitality Services did not have a website. The goal was to give the business a clear, professional online presence that it could share with prospective customers.

The project moved through an iterative design process, with approximately 8–10 design samples and rounds of refinement before the final direction was selected. Once approved, the website was developed and brought live in approximately one month, with the launch timed around Ganpati in 2026.

The work focused on understanding the business, shaping the site structure, refining the design with the client and delivering a responsive, 23-page website with SEO-ready foundations.

Starting pointNo existing website
Design process8–10 iterations
DeliveryApprox. one month
01 / Understand

Learn the business and what it needed to communicate to potential customers.

02 / Refine

Explore multiple design directions and work through feedback until the direction felt right.

03 / Deliver

Build the approved 23-page website and prepare it for its public launch.
