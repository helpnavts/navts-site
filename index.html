/ 04 — Common questions

What is NAVTS?

NAVTS is an independent technology company that creates products, builds technology solutions for clients and explores emerging technologies.

What kind of products does NAVTS make?

Our product scope is intentionally broad. It can include games, apps, SaaS platforms, AI tools, enterprise software, consumer electronics, computers, smart devices, appliances and future technology categories.

Does NAVTS still provide website services?

Yes. Website design, development, domain and hosting setup, SEO and maintenance remain an active part of our services business.

Can NAVTS build a custom solution for my business?

We welcome enquiries about websites, software, applications, automation and other technology projects. Share your requirements and we can discuss scope and feasibility.

Where is NAVTS based?

NAVTS is based in India and can discuss projects with customers beyond India as well.
