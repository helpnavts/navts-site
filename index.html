/ 04 — Common questions

Small business website design,
answered clearly.

How much does a small business website cost in India?

It depends on pages, features and how much custom design you need. A professional business website typically includes design, development, mobile-responsive layout, domain/hosting setup and SEO groundwork. Share your brief on WhatsApp or email and we’ll quote a clear scope.

Do I need a website for my small business?

Yes, if you want customers to find you on Google, trust your brand, and enquire without relying only on WhatsApp forwards. A professional website is your 24/7 storefront — especially for local businesses in Solapur, Maharashtra and across India.

What is an SEO-ready website?

An SEO-ready website is built so search engines can understand it: clean URLs, page titles, meta descriptions, headings, sitemap, schema markup, fast loading and mobile-friendly design. NAVTS includes this technical SEO from day one — not as an afterthought.

How long does website design and development take?

Simple business websites can launch in a few weeks. Larger sites, like a multi-page hospitality website, take longer because of content, structure and SEO. We’ll give you a realistic timeline after we see your pages and goals.

Do you offer website hosting, domain and maintenance?

Yes. NAVTS handles domain registration, website hosting, launch setup and ongoing website maintenance so your site stays live, updated and useful after go-live.
