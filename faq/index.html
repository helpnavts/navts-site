About NAVTS

NAVTS is an independent technology company that creates products, builds technology solutions for clients and explores emerging technologies.

What is the vision behind NAVTS?

Technology Without Boundaries. We build what matters today while staying open to what becomes possible tomorrow. NAVTS is designed to grow across product categories and technology services without being limited to one industry.

What is the difference between Products and Services?

Products are things NAVTS creates and develops as its own portfolio. Services are technology solutions NAVTS delivers for clients, such as websites, software development and technology consulting.

What product categories does NAVTS explore?

Our portfolio can include games and entertainment, applications, SaaS and cloud platforms, AI products and agents, enterprise software, devices and electronics, and emerging technologies.

Are all listed product categories currently available?

No. Some categories describe future product directions rather than products currently for sale. Each product or category page should clearly state its actual status and availability.

What games does NAVTS make?

AFTERGLOW is our first featured laser-and-mirror puzzle game. It is part of the Games portfolio, where future titles will also appear.

Does NAVTS make physical products?

Physical products are part of the long-term vision. This may include computers, laptops, consumer electronics, appliances and connected devices. These are future product possibilities, not claims that every category is currently manufactured or sold.

What services does NAVTS currently offer?

Website design and development, domain and hosting setup, SEO-ready foundations and website maintenance are core services. Software, app development, AI integration and consulting enquiries can be discussed based on project scope and feasibility.

Can NAVTS build custom software for my business?

You can contact us to discuss software, web and mobile applications, integrations, AI workflows and other technology requirements. Scope, feasibility, timing and deliverables are agreed before work begins.

Where is NAVTS based?

NAVTS is based in Pune, Maharashtra, India, and can discuss projects with customers in other markets.

How does NAVTS approach a project?

We begin by understanding the requirement, agree on scope and practical expectations, develop with feedback and prepare a considered handover. Ongoing support can be discussed where needed.

How do I contact NAVTS?

Use the contact page to send an enquiry through WhatsApp or email. Share a short description of your requirement and we can take it from there.
