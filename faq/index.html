What is NAVTS?

NAVTS is an independent technology company that creates products, builds technology solutions for clients and explores emerging technologies.

What kind of products can NAVTS create?

The product portfolio is intentionally broad. It can include games, applications, SaaS platforms, AI tools, enterprise software, consumer electronics, computers, laptops, appliances, refrigerators, smart-home devices and future technologies.

Are physical products part of the NAVTS vision?

Yes. NAVTS is not limited to digital products. Physical products such as mobile phones, computers, laptops, appliances and connected devices can fit within the product portfolio if and when we develop or bring them to market.

Does NAVTS currently manufacture all these products?

No. These are potential future product families, not a claim that every category is currently available. We will present individual products when they are developed and ready to share.

Does NAVTS still offer website design and development?

Yes. Website design, development, domain and hosting setup, SEO-ready foundations and website maintenance remain active services.

What website services are available?

Custom responsive website design, web development, domain registration guidance, hosting and launch setup, technical SEO foundations and ongoing website maintenance.

Can NAVTS build software for my business?

You can contact us to discuss software, web and mobile applications, integrations, AI workflows and other technology requirements. Scope and feasibility are discussed for each project.

Where is NAVTS based?

NAVTS is based in Pune, Maharashtra, India, and can discuss projects with customers in other markets.

Who is behind NAVTS?

NAVTS presents itself as a company brand. We keep the public-facing focus on the work, products, values and customer experience rather than individual identities.

How does NAVTS approach a project?

We begin by understanding the requirement, agree on scope and practical expectations, develop with feedback and prepare a considered handover. Ongoing support can be discussed where needed.

How do I contact NAVTS?

Use the contact page to send an enquiry through WhatsApp or email. Share a short description of your requirement and we can take it from there.
